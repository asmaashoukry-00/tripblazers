The Legend Unfolds

Reception

Meet and greet at Queen Alia International Airport. Transfer to your hotel for dinner and overnight stay in the heart of Jordan's capital.

Umayyad Heritage

Explore Qusayr Amra (UNESCO site), Qasr Kharana, and Qasr al-Azraq. Visit the ruins of Qasr al-'Abed before returning to Amman.

Imperial Grandeur

Walk through the Roman ruins of Jerash and visit Ajloun Castle. Conclude with a city tour of the Citadel and the historic Amman bazaar.

The King's Highway

Visit the mosaic city of Madaba and Mount Nebo. Explore the Crusader architecture of Kerak Castle before arriving in Petra.

Rose-Red Magic

A full guided tour through the Siq to the Treasury, Royal Tombs, and funerary chambers. A deep dive into Nabataean mysteries.

Valley of the Moon

Visit Beida (Little Petra), then depart to the enchanting desert of Wadi Rum for a 2-hour 4x4 tour and Bedouin hospitality.

Total Rejuvenation

Travel to the lowest point on Earth. Free time for swimming and experiencing the healing mineral-rich waters of the Dead Sea.

Safe Travels

Breakfast at the hotel followed by a transfer to Queen Alia International Airport (QAIA) for your flight home.
