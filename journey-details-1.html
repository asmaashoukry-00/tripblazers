The Unfolding Story

Queen Alia International Airport

Meet and greet upon arrival at Queen Alia International Airport. Our representatives will provide full assistance through logistics and transfer you to your hotel in the capital for check-in, a warm dinner, and your first overnight stay in Jordan.

Ancient ruins & Capital culture

Embark on a guided tour of the Jerash ruins, one of the best-preserved Roman provincial towns in the world. Later, enjoy a comprehensive Amman walking tour, exploring the historic Citadel and the heartbeat of the modern city.

Madaba - Mt. Nebo - Dead Sea

Explore the intricate mosaics of Madaba and take in the profound views from Mount Nebo. Conclude your day with ultimate relaxation, floating in the mineral-rich, healing waters of the Dead Sea.

Dana Biosphere Reserve

Journey to the Dana Biosphere Reserve. Witness the raw beauty of Jordan’s diverse ecology through short guided hikes and breathtaking scenic viewpoints overlooking the ancient rift valley.

UNESCO World Heritage Site

A full day immersion in the Nabataean capital. Walk through the Siq to behold the Treasury, explore the Royal Tombs, and for the adventurous, take the optional hike to the majestic Monastery.

The Valley of the Moon

Venture into the Martian landscapes of Wadi Rum. Enjoy a professional Jeep tour, capture the desert at sunset, and choose an optional camel trek for a truly authentic Bedouin experience.

Aqaba Coastline

Conclude your journey in Aqaba. Spend your morning at leisure by the Red Sea, perhaps snorkeling in the coral reefs, before your private transfer to the airport for your final departure.
