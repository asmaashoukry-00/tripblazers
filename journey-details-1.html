The Narrative

Upon arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our dedicated concierge will greet you at the bridge. Experience a seamless VIP transfer to your luxury residence in Amman for a private dinner and rest.

Step into the past at Jerash, the "Pompeii of the East," exploring perfectly preserved colonnaded streets. Later, witness the sunset from the Amman Citadel, where the echoes of Umayyad and Roman history converge.

Discover the intricate Madaba mosaics and stand atop Mount Nebo for a panoramic view of the Promised Land. End the day by floating in the therapeutic, mineral-rich waters of the Dead Sea.

Journey south to the Dana Biosphere Reserve. This is where nature breathes; experience a guided walk through ancient geology and diverse wildlife before settling into a night of quiet luxury.

A full day dedicated to Petra. Traverse the Siq to reveal the Treasury, and let our historian guide you through the Royal Tombs and the Monastery, uncovering the secrets of the Nabataean Kingdom.

Experience the raw beauty of Wadi Rum via a private 4x4 expedition. Dine under a canopy of stars in an authentic Bedouin setting, where the desert silence tells its own story.

Awake in Aqaba. Enjoy a final morning of leisure by the crystal waters of the Red Sea before our team assists with your transfer to the airport for your journey home.
