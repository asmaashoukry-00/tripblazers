The Detailed Itinerary

Experience the seamless transition into Jordanian hospitality. Upon arrival at Queen Alia Airport, our concierge team handles your logistics while you prepare for a refined evening in Amman's finest quarters.

Discover the "Pompeii of the East" at Jerash, where Roman colonnaded streets remain frozen in time. Conclude the day at the Amman Citadel, witnessing a sunset that bridges the gap between antiquity and the modern capital.

From the mosaics of Madaba to the panoramic views from Mount Nebo. Finish your day at the Dead Sea, rejuvenating in mineral-rich waters at the lowest point on Earth.

Venture into the Dana Biosphere Reserve. Witness the raw beauty of Jordan’s diverse ecology and ancient geology, a perfect prelude to the desert wonders awaiting you.

A full day immersion in the UNESCO World Heritage site. Walk through the Siq to behold the Treasury and explore the sprawling tombs of the Nabataean Empire.

4x4 exploration of the Martian landscapes of Wadi Rum. An authentic Bedouin experience followed by a night under a canopy of desert stars.

Conclude in Aqaba by the crystal waters of the Red Sea. Enjoy leisure time for snorkeling or relaxation before our team transfers you for your final departure.
