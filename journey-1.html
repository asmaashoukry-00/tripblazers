Experience the perfect balance of culture, history, and relaxation. Designed especially for travelers from Belgium seeking a meaningful and comfortable Middle East journey.

From the Roman ruins of Jerash to the therapeutic Dead Sea and the rose-red magic of Petra, conclude your adventure by the crystal-clear waters of Aqaba.

The Itinerary

Meet & Greet

Arrival at Queen Alia International Airport. Assistance by our representatives and transfer to your hotel. Dinner and overnight stay in the capital.

Roman Heritage

A guided tour of the beautifully preserved ruins of Jerash, followed by a walking tour of Amman's historic Citadel.

Mosaics & Floating

Explore the famous mosaics of Madaba and the viewpoint of Mount Nebo before heading to the Dead Sea for a rejuvenating float.

Nature & Hikes

Immerse yourself in nature with short guided hikes and breathtaking scenic viewpoints at the Dana Reserve.

The Rose-Red City

Explore the Treasury, Siq, and Royal Tombs. Optional hike to the Monastery for those seeking the ultimate viewpoint.

Martian Landscapes

A 2-hour 4x4 jeep tour through the dramatic dunes, optional camel trek, and sunset photography in the heart of the desert.

Red Sea Leisure

Enjoy morning snorkeling or relaxation by the Red Sea before your transfer for final departure.
